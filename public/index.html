Anabaptist Aid Initiative

Anabaptist Aid Initiative (AAI) is a registered Nigerian organization that helps coordinate SALT Savings Groups, Youth Savings Groups, and vocational training in Nigeria.

AAI works with local facilitators and communities in Plateau, Borno, Bauchi, Adamawa, and nearby states.

Who we are

Purpose

Support SALT work in Nigerian communities through Christian teaching, savings group discipline, and practical skills training.

What we do

Help organize Savings Groups, Youth Savings Groups, and Living Hope Vocational School activities with local field teams.

Local staff and facilitators

Teaching, organizing, and walking with groups

AAI's work depends on local staff and facilitators who teach lessons, help organize meetings, and support Savings Groups, Youth Savings Groups, and vocational classes.

The emphasis is steady local service rather than a separate program office speaking from far away.

AAI, SALT Nigeria, BHN, and CAM

AAI coordinates local SALT (Shared Accountability, Lending, and Teaching) program work in Nigeria. The work is connected with Brethren Hope for the Nations (BHN) and Christian Aid Ministries (CAM) through the broader SALT Nigeria effort. AAI's role is practical local coordination: working with field teams, communities, and reporting channels in Nigeria.

Current work

Savings groups

SALT Savings Groups and Youth Savings Groups meet in several Nigerian states.

The focus is regular saving, accountability, and basic business teaching.

Vocational training

Living Hope Vocational School training includes practical trades such as carpentry, tailoring, bagmaking, and shoemaking.

The emphasis is hands-on skill development that can be practiced locally.
