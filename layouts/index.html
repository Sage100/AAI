Anabaptist Aid Initiative

Anabaptist Aid Initiative (AAI) is the registered Nigerian organization that coordinates SALT Savings Groups, Youth Savings Groups, and vocational training in Nigeria.

The SALT program work AAI coordinates began in early 2017 and continues with local facilitators in Plateau, Borno, Bauchi, Adamawa, and nearby states.

Local staff and facilitators

Teaching, organizing, and walking with groups

AAI's work depends on local staff and facilitators who teach lessons, help organize meetings, and support Savings Groups, Youth Savings Groups, and vocational classes.

AAI, SALT Nigeria, BHN, and CAM

Anabaptist Aid Initiative is the Nigerian side of this work. The lessons and materials groups use carry the SALT (Savings and Loan Teaching) program name, and the work is guided in America by Brethren Hope for the Nations (BHN), working on behalf of Christian Aid Ministries (CAM). In Nigeria, AAI handles the practical coordination: field teams, communities, and reporting.

Current work

Savings groups

SALT Savings Groups and Youth Savings Groups meet in several Nigerian states.

The focus is regular saving, accountability, and basic business teaching.

Vocational training

Living Hope Vocational School training includes practical trades such as carpentry, tailoring, bag making, and shoemaking.

The emphasis is hands-on skill development that can be practiced locally.
