SALT program work in Nigeria

The SALT Program

AAI helps coordinate SALT (Shared Accountability, Lending, and Teaching) program activities in Nigeria, including Savings Groups, Youth Savings Groups, and vocational training.

Program pillars Contact AAI

Core components

  • Savings Groups Members meet regularly, save together, contribute to a shared fund, and receive Bible-based teaching and basic business instruction.
  • Vocational Training (LHVS) Living Hope Vocational School provides practical training in trades such as carpentry, tailoring, bagmaking, and shoemaking.
  • Youth Savings Groups Youth groups introduce younger members to saving, basic business teaching, and Christian instruction.
  • Business Teaching Lessons cover practical topics such as pricing, record keeping, customer service, and planning.
  • Group Support Facilitators work with group leaders and help keep meetings organized.

How AAI serves locally

AAI helps coordinate SALT activities in Nigeria with local facilitators, churches, community leaders, and field teams.

The work is connected with Brethren Hope for the Nations and Christian Aid Ministries through the broader SALT Nigeria effort.
